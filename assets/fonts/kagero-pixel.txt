# Each glyph is "[char]" (or "[U+XXXX]", "[space]") followed by its rows.
# The first 8 rows sit on the baseline (row 1 = cap height); rows 9–10 are
# descenders and may be omitted. The build draws rows 2, 6 and 7 twice (11-pixel
# capitals) and one interior column of most glyphs twice (see build-font.py). "#" is a pixel, "." is empty. The glyph width is
# the row length; one empty column is added after every glyph.
